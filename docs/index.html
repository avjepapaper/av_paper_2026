Overview

This page collects the audio-visual examples referenced in the paper. Play videos with sound: most examples can only be judged by watching and listening together.

AV-JEPA

One encoder for video, raw audio, or both, trained with intra- and cross-modal ProtoLoss on 500 ms chunks.

AVJ-WM

A causal audio-visual world model on the frozen AV-JEPA encoder, with action adapters for planning.

IntPhys-X

Cross-modal intuitive physics: cross-modal matching and cross-modal in-context learning.

AVNav-Bench

Audio-visual navigation to image and sound goals, offline and in closed loop.

IntPhys-X: cross-modal matching

A query in one modality comes with two options in the other; exactly one stems from the same physical event. In v2a items a silent video is matched to one of two soundtracks; in a2v items a soundtrack is matched to one of two silent videos.

Each example: the query, option A and option B, and the answer (hidden behind a toggle so the reader can try first). Synthetic and real (recorded) versions where both exist.

IntPhys-X: cross-modal in-context learning (long context)

A context clip with picture and sound binds sounds to visual events; an audio-only query continues it, and the matching video option must be chosen. Scenarios increase in difficulty.
